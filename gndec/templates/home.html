180nm CMOS Inverter — SPICE Characterization IISc CeNSE
June 2026 LTSpice · Ngspice · BSIM3v3 Python analysis
Problem

Characterize DC/transient performance of a 180nm bulk CMOS inverter and optimize Wp/Wn sizing for noise margin and delay.

Approach

Simulated 4 Wp/Wn configurations using LTSpice/Ngspice with BSIM3v3 models. Extracted VTC, noise margins, propagation delay, and dynamic power.

Result

Identified 2:1 sizing as optimal — NML 0.643V, NMH 0.898V, delay 40.1 ps, dynamic power 3.24 nW @ 100 MHz, PDP 0.29 fJ.
